# NASA harness-workmanship source pages

Recorded October21,2026 by Codex (OpenAI). NASA-STD-8739.4A, Change4 (2022-04-13),
*Workmanship Standard for Crimping, Interconnecting Cables, Harnesses, and Wiring*.
[NASA active standard record](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-87394).
[Original public PDF](https://standards.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/standards/NASA/A/4/nasa-std-87394a_w_change_4_0.pdf).

Saved complete original pages29-31 as `nasa-std8739-4a-c4-029.jpg` through031.jpg,
mechanically rendered with Poppler and visually checked. Credit: NASA. Source is a standard,
not a Dragonfly equipment photograph or proof of that project's implementation.

- Page29: connector stress relief and wiring support, paragraphs7.2.19-7.2.33.
- Page30: Table7-1 differentiates bend radii by cable construction. The simulated RDE
  cable construction is unknown; no row is asserted applicable and no compliant radius claimed.
- Page31: permanent offsets/bends and connector backshell accommodation, paragraphs8.1.2/8.1.3.

Used for general design reasoning only. Four RDE slack bows, diameter, lacing count, material
appearance and routing remain estimates. This is not qualification, manufacturing advice or
a NASA-approved flight harness model. No pins, circuit assignments or internal splices drawn.

Real Dragonfly appearance reference remains the original September2026 NASA/APL/Ed Whitman
wiring photograph saved in `research/nose-bulkhead-2026-10-19/`, with the existing close crop
under `research/main-harness-2026-10-21/`. That image does not identify exact RDE connector runs.
