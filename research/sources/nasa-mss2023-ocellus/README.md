# NASA Ocellus Design Reference

Farzin Amzajerdian (NASA Langley), *An Overview of NASA Lidar Technologies for Precision
Safe Landing on Planetary Bodies*, 2023 MSS Active E-O Systems/E-O & IRCM Conference.
[NTRS record 20230004117](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230004117), public US Government work.
[Original PDF](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230004117/downloads/MSS%202023_Landing%20Lidar%20Overview.pdf).

`mss2023-page06.png` is original PDF page 6, rendered at 2600 px by Codex on October 19, 2026.
The PDF stays outside Git. Its illustrations are **CAD**, not real hardware photographs.
Ocellus PI credited on the slide: Nathaniel Gill, NASA Goddard.

Used for #05 nominal 2023 design dimensions: MEB 24.19 x 18.54 x 12.04 cm; FEB 23.4 x
23.2 x 12.7 cm. These are not verified current flight dimensions. The listed masses are
CBE estimates, not new simulator mass constants. The schedule is historical, not current.
The larger Kodiak boxes on the same slide must not be substituted for Ocellus.

Dimensions are [PUB-design]; orientation in the vehicle, detailed housing construction,
materials/coatings, screws, feet, optical interfaces and installed coordinates are not
established by this reference. No internal boards/optics are copied from its schematic CAD.
