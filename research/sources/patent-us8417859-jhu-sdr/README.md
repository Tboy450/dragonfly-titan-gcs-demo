# Patent US 8,417,859 B2: Modular Software-Defined Radios (Johns Hopkins University)

Haskins, C. B., Millard, W. P. *Low power, low mass, modular, multi-band software-defined
radios.* US 8,417,859 B2, assigned to Johns Hopkins University. Filed September 29, 2009
(provisional September 30, 2008), granted April 9, 2013. Made with U.S. Government support.
[Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8417859B2/en),
[PDF](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/c7/a4/b1/37d0ef2dee6403/US8417859.pdf)

The radio is built from separate receiver slices, exciter slices and a digital signal
processor that plug into connector planes. The patent does not name Frontier, but its inventors
are APL radio engineers (Millard co-wrote the Frontier Radio Lite paper) and it describes the
same slice-based design. It shows block diagrams, not the box's mechanical design.
