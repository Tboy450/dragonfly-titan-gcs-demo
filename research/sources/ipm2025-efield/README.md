Figure 2 is the actual two-assembly engineering-model photograph used for appearance:
gold electrode balls, black stalks, copper-colored guards, low preamp enclosures and
co-mounted atmospheric temperature/microphone devices. Figure 1 is CAD used only for
location. Neither the crossed test-stand pose nor the calibration chamber is vehicle hardware.

[PUB]: two fixed probes; 30 cm carbon-fiber composite stalk; 5 cm two-piece gold-plated
aluminum electrode; conductive stalk driven with guard voltage. One upper starboard front,
one lower rear near port skid and MMRTG. The preamp operates in Titan's atmosphere and
the EVPS slice is in the internal DPU. No hidden boards are reconstructed in the app.
Exact preamp sizes, mounting coordinates/angles, stalk diameter, tabs and harnesses are [EST].
Older Chatain et al. 2022/2023 EFIELD work is background, not the source of the newer sizes.
