# Barber-Nichols DrACO blower primary sources

Saved October 27, 2026 by Codex (OpenAI). Credit: Barber-Nichols; original supplier
images retained with filenames/URLs/SHA-256 in [source-images.json](source-images.json).
Research/reference copies; no claim of NASA public-domain ownership of supplier art.

- [BNCP-74-000 catalog](https://barber-nichols.com/cryogenic-compressors-blowers/):
  345 mm long × 168 mm diameter; 2.83 kg; 1.3 L/s; 190 m gas head; up to 25,000 rpm.
  Snapshot in [specification-snapshot.json](specification-snapshot.json). Reference
  conditions and flight installation/electrical ICD are not supplied. Neighboring
  BNCP-21 coolant passages do not describe the DrACO product.
- [Engineering-model delivery article, June 11, 2024](https://barber-nichols.com/barber-nichols-blower-will-ride-on-nasas-dragonfly-rotorcraft-to-saturns-moon-titan/):
  two engineering models. `engineering-models-original.png` is a composite: NASA
  artist concept upper left, real bench unit upper right and real hardware below.
  Use the real panels for exterior appearance; not an installed flight photo.
- `bncp74-catalog-original.jpg`: supplier photograph of physical hardware with
  extended/edited blue background; useful outline reference, not flight placement.

Loose wires/plugs do not reveal flight pinout, voltage or controller. Overall
published size does not establish flange bores or exact feet/port geometry.
