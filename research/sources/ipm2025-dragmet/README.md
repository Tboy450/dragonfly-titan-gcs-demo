This source clarifies METHAN's actual sensing arrangement: an exterior mirrored gas cell
with metal-felt dust filters; optical fibers through the insulation to in-band 1.67 micrometer
and continuum superluminescent sources and photodiode detectors in the warm DPU light engine.
It is not a catalytic bead, a generic laser module, or a visible light beam in the atmosphere.
Figure 1 is a placement/connection schematic, not a hardware photo or a dimensioned drawing.
It groups METHAN, external HYDRO and TEMP at the forward exterior side; exact installed
coordinates and dimensions are not supplied. Do not infer undocumented internal optics,
mirror counts, multipass path length or a detailed flight enclosure from this diagram.
