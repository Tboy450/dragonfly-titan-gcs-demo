# Dragonfly body-insulation evidence

Saved and visually reviewed October 22, 2026 by Codex (OpenAI).

## Real test hardware, September 2025

- [NASA article, September 8, 2025](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/dragonfly/2025/09/08/nasas-dragonfly-soaring-through-key-development-test-activities/).
- [Original image](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/missions/dragonfly/DragonCam%20Insulation.png),
  saved unaltered as `dragoncam-insulation-test.png`.
- Credit: **Johns Hopkins APL / Justin Artis**, as NASA's article credits it.
- Real foam test segment being prepared by Mozany Sosmath. Orange encapsulation and
  machined contours are observable; this is not a complete installed flight body.
- Article identifies nominal 3-inch (7.6 cm) **Solimide-based** body insulation.
  Do not infer an exact installed tile map, local thickness or universal orange finish.

## Foam development, May 2026

- [NASA NESC presentation landing page](https://nescacademy.nasa.gov/video/d09e3ab012a7423881c9b253ecb718931d).
- [Official slides](https://nescacademy.nasa.gov/downloadFile/1529).
- Rolando Rodarte, Johns Hopkins APL; NASA NESC. Slide footers/PDF metadata identify
  May 14, 2026; slide 3 retains a September 2025 footer. The landing page's generic
  older last-updated field is not the presentation date.
- Saved rendered pages: 3, 6, 11, 12, 13, with embedded credits/labels retained.
  Embedded hardware photos are real; slide 3 also contains **CAD, not a photograph**.
  No individual photographer is inferred for uncredited embedded photos.
- Slide 3: roughly 800 tiles, covering most external surfaces; thermal resistance purpose.
- Slides 6/11: open-cell Solimide 16 remains flexible under cryogenic testing; selected
  over candidate Rohacell despite the latter's lower tested conductivity.
- Slide 11 test table: Solimide 16 density 16 kg/m3, effective conductivity .019 W/m/K
  at 1.5 atm and approximately .002 W/m/K in vacuum. These are **test values**, not
  a universal material law or calibrated simulator constants.
- Slide 12: feature-size machining/coupon testing. Slide 13: two encapsulation approaches
  depend on structural load path; depressurization/venting and contamination/bakeout tests.
  A vent-grid sizing example is shown; it is not an as-built flight-hole map.

## Limits and revision handling

Earlier 2020/2023 Rohacell references remain historical evidence. This newer body choice
does not identify the material in every local antenna/camera insulator or HGA core.
No material conductivity, thermal conductance, mass, temperatures or controller is
changed by the October 22 surface-region asset pass. Audit the legacy thermal assumptions
separately with the selected-material test conditions and complete installation stack.
No proprietary information, hidden membrane layup, adhesive pattern or flight tile cuts
are reconstructed. No image generation was used.
