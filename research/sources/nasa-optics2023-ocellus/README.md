The labeled FEB figure is CAD: base, round optical assembly, angled structure and other
components are visible. The slide discusses ongoing packaging, collimator modeling and
alignment work. It is a historical design, NOT an as-built hardware photo or dimensioned
manufacturing drawing. CAD colors do not establish real coatings/materials. The red circle
is a presentation annotation, not hardware. The lander pictures are concepts, not photos.

Use for identifying design arrangement/relationships only under a clearly labeled approved
approximation, not a hidden optical path, exact component dimensions, connector pinouts,
photographic finish or a verified flight installation. The 2023 overall dimensions come
from separate NASA MSS p6, not this perspective illustration. Do not copy internal optical
details merely to fill empty space or treat a protective cover as an established flight lid.
