  coverage is not evidence of radiation, vibration, bonding or spaceflight qualification.
- The [manufacturer web page](https://mx.omega.com/pptst_eng/F1500_F2000_F4000.html)
  gives a general -50..500 C statement depending on class. Both versions cover157 C; do not
  silently combine a lower operating limit or an accuracy class from different sheets.
- Real F2020 photograph: small square element, dark blue glass region, paired metal leads.
  Proportions of glass versus ceramic, reflectivity and exact exit geometry remain [EST].
  F4050/F2010 photos are related family context, not scale substitutes for F2020.
