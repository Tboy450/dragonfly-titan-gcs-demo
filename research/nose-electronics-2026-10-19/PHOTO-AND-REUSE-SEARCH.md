## 1. Honeywell Space MIMU: Stronger IMU Hardware-Family Lead

- [Honeywell, Jeremy Ludes, People Behind the Product, April 20, 2021](https://www.honeywellaerospace.com/us/en/insights/articles/people-behind-the-product):
  discusses the space MIMU used for Mars 2020 and refers to the customer's Dragonfly
  development. This is a meaningful supplier/family connection, NOT proof of the exact
  final Dragonfly part number, revision, mounting or delivered flight configuration.
- [Honeywell space MIMU product page](https://www.honeywellaerospace.com/us/en/products-and-services/products/navigation-and-sensors/inertial-measurement-units/miniature-inertial-measurement-unit):
  clear product image and published family envelope under 233.7 mm diameter x 169.9 mm
  height. Do not treat this maximum envelope as an exact housing diameter or import its
  power/mass into Dragonfly's simulator without mission-specific evidence.
- [Original supplier product image](https://honeywellaerospace.scene7.com/is/image/honeywellaerospaceassets/hon-aero-mimu-banner).
  Credit Honeywell Aerospace. Manufacturer product imagery, not a Dragonfly installation
  photograph; exact image provenance/retouching cannot be established from the caption.
- [Honeywell datasheet N61-1613-000-000, 03/21](https://aerospace.honeywell.com/content/dam/aerobt/en/documents/learn/products/space/datasheet/N61-1613-000-000_MIMU-ds.pdf):
  published budgets and labeled exploded design view. An exploded illustration is NOT
  a real interior photograph. Distinguish IMU/velocity-channel options from gyro-only IRU.
- [NASA Uranus study Appendix C](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/uranus-orbiter-and-probe-appendix-c.pdf):
  search-index excerpt lists Honeywell MIMU alongside New Horizons and Dragonfly. Full PDF
  did not open in this session, so treat this as a corroboration LEAD, not an inspected
  flight-hardware source. "Flight heritage" on a study slide does not mean Dragonfly has flown.

## 2. Ocellus/Kodiak/SQRLi: Reuse Is Documented, Not Identical Boxes

- [NASA TechPort HTRL/SQRLi project 116315](https://techport.nasa.gov/projects/116315):
  the project description identifies SpaceCube Mini processing in the Kodiak and Ocellus
  MEBs. Confirms processor-family reuse, not an exact board revision or identical housings.
- [NASA MSS 2023 p6](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230004117), already saved locally:
  Ocellus leverages Kodiak and was developed with SQRLi on a common platform; their
  separately labeled box envelopes differ. The picture is CAD. Do not copy Kodiak's
  larger enclosure, connector layout or optics unchanged.
- [NASA IGARSS 2020 lidar presentation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20205007653/downloads/IGARSS%202020%20%2809JUL20%29r%5B2%5D.pdf),
  page 43: Kodiak's MEB relates to GRLR/GRSSLi with modifications. This is heritage, not
  evidence of Ocellus's final internal layout. Kodiak's stated laser wavelength differs
  from the Ocellus 2023 specification: do not transfer the old optical train wholesale.
- [NASA SQRLi 2024 annual review, Nathaniel Gill](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240010518):
  public PPTX, `FY24 GCD APR - HTRL SQRLi Required Only 20240814a.pptx`.
  Promising newer hardware-photo lead. File could not be opened with web tools, so its
  slides/images have NOT been inspected and must not be described as verified evidence.
